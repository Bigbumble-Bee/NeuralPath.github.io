Coefficients & Constants

Let's take an algebraic expression apart and see what each piece is doing.

Visual 01 · Take it apart

Look at the expression.

Here's a simple algebraic expression: 3x + 5. Each part has a different job.

3 coefficient
x variable
+
5 constant
3 tells us how many x's we have. x is the variable. 5 is a number that stays by itself. These pieces work together to make the expression 3x + 5.
Visual 02 · Coefficient

Think of the coefficient as a multiplier.

In 4x, the 4 tells us how many x's we have.

4x
x
x
x
x
4x means: x + x + x + x
The coefficient is 4. It tells us how many times the variable is being used.
Visual 03 · Constant

A constant stands on its own.

A constant is a number without a variable attached to it.

7
Nothing is attached to the 7.
It simply stays 7.
Because its value does not depend on a variable. In the expression 4x + 7, the 7 remains 7.
Visual 04 · Compare them

Coefficient vs. constant

They are both numbers, but they play different roles.

4x

Coefficient

Connected to a variable. It tells us how many times the variable is being used.

7

Constant

Stands alone. It has its own fixed value.
