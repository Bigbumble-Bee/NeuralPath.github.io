Multi-Step Equations

You've already learned how to undo one operation at a time. Now we're going to combine those skills to solve equations with several steps.

Algebra problem solving 1 of 4
Step 1 · The plan

Don't solve everything at once.

Multi-step equations look complicated because several operations are happening at the same time.

The trick is to break the problem into smaller decisions.

01 Distribute
02 Combine like terms
03 Move variables
04 Move constants
05 Isolate x
06 Check
NeuralPath tip You do NOT always need every step. Use the steps that actually appear in the equation.
Step 2 · Follow along

Let's solve one together.

Try not to jump ahead. Follow each transformation one at a time.

3x + 5 = 20
1
Look for the variable. The variable is x. The number 3 is multiplying x, and 5 is being added.
2
Undo addition first. Subtract 5 from both sides.
3x + 5 − 5 = 20 − 5
3
Simplify.
3x = 15
4
Undo multiplication. Divide both sides by 3.
x = 5
x = 5
Step 3 · Distribute

What if there are parentheses?

Sometimes the first thing you need to do is distribute.

Multiply the number outside the parentheses by every term inside.

2(x + 3) = 14
1
Multiply 2 by x.
2x
2
Multiply 2 by 3.
6
3
Rewrite the equation.
2x + 6 = 14
4
Subtract 6.
2x = 8
5
Divide by 2.
x = 4
Watch the parentheses. If you have: −3(x + 2) the negative 3 multiplies BOTH terms.
Step 4 · Combine

Put like terms together.

After distributing, you may have several terms that can be combined.

2x + 3x + 4 = 24
1
Find the like terms.
2x + 3x
2
Combine them.
5x + 4 = 24
3
Continue solving.
5x = 20
4
Divide by 5.
x = 4
Step 5 · Level up

What if x is on both sides?

This looks intimidating, but the goal is still exactly the same: get x by itself.

5x + 2 = 2x + 14
1
Move the variable terms. Subtract 2x from both sides.
3x + 2 = 14
2
Move the constant. Subtract 2.
3x = 12
3
Divide by 3.
x = 4
Step 6 · Check

Don't skip the check.

One of the easiest ways to catch a mistake is to substitute your answer back into the original equation.

5x + 2 = 2x + 14

We found:

x = 4

Substitute 4 for x.

5(4) + 2 = 2(4) + 14
20 + 2 = 8 + 14
22 = 22

Both sides are equal, so the answer checks out.

Need another way?

There isn't one "correct" way to understand a math problem. Try the explanation that fits your brain.

Keep it simple

Think of an equation like a locked box. Your job is to undo everything around x until only x is left. Work one operation at a time.

Show me visually

Imagine both sides of the equation sitting on a balance scale. Whatever you do to one side, you must do to the other.

Give me a checklist

□ Distribute
□ Combine like terms
□ Move variable terms
□ Move constants
□ Isolate x
□ Check your answer

Explain it in words

Instead of thinking about six separate algebra rules, think: "I am cleaning up the equation until the variable is alone."

You've got the idea

Multi-step equations: complete.

You can now take an equation with several operations and break it into manageable pieces.

Distribute → Combine → Move → Isolate → Check

Keep this pattern in mind as equations become more complicated.
