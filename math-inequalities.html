Algebra · Foundations

Inequalities

Equations tell us that two things are equal. Inequalities tell us that one value is greater than or less than another.

Algebra foundations 5 of 5
Step 1 · Understand

Meet the inequality symbols.

These symbols compare two values. Think of them as telling you which side is larger.

< less than
> greater than
≤ less than or equal to
≥ greater than or equal to
3 < 8

This means: 3 is less than 8.

Step 2 · Visualize

Think on a number line.

Numbers get larger as you move to the right and smaller as you move to the left.

1 2 3 4 5

An open circle means the endpoint is not included. A closed circle means the endpoint is included.

x > 3

So the solutions are all numbers greater than 3.

Step 3 · Solve

Solving an inequality

Solving an inequality works almost exactly like solving an equation.

x + 4 > 9
1
Identify what is being done to x.
x + 4
2
Undo addition by subtracting 4 from both sides.
x + 4 − 4 > 9 − 4
3
Simplify.
x > 5
x > 5
Step 4 · Important

There is one special rule.

When you multiply or divide both sides by a negative number, the inequality symbol reverses.

Remember this:

Multiply or divide by a negative → flip the sign.
−2x > 8
1
Divide both sides by −2.
x < −4
2
Notice that the symbol changed from > to <.
Step 5 · Practice

Your turn.

Solve the inequality:

x + 3 > 7

Which answer is correct?

One more:

x − 5 ≤ 2

Challenge:

−3x > 12

Need another way?

Choose the explanation that works best for you.

Keep it simple

Solve an inequality like an equation. Undo the operation on x. The answer may contain many numbers, not just one.

Show me visually

Imagine the number line. Greater-than points toward the right. Less-than points toward the left. Open circle = don't include the number. Closed circle = include the number.

Explain it in words

x > 5 means: "x can be any number greater than 5." That could be 6, 7, 100, 5.5, or infinitely many other numbers.

Explain the negative rule

Multiplying or dividing by a negative reverses the order of numbers. That's why the inequality symbol has to flip.
