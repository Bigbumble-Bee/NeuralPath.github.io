One-Step
Equations

An equation is like a balance. Your goal is to figure out what value makes both sides equal.

Lesson progress 4 of 5
Step 1 · Understand

What is an equation?

An equation says that two expressions have the same value.

x + 4 = 9

We don't know what x is yet. But we know that when 4 is added to x, the result is 9.

Think of a balance.

The two sides of the equation must stay equal. Whatever you do to one side, you must also do to the other side.

Step 2 · Understand

Undo what was done.

To isolate the variable, use the inverse operation. In other words: do the opposite.

Addition

+ 4

is undone by

− 4

Multiplication

× 4

is undone by

÷ 4
x + 4 = 9

Since 4 was added to x, subtract 4 from both sides.

Step 3 · Solve

Let's solve it together.

1
Start with the equation:
x + 4 = 9
2
Subtract 4 from both sides:
x + 4 − 4 = 9 − 4
3
Simplify:
x = 5
x = 5

We found the value of x. The answer is 5.

Step 4 · Practice

Your turn.

Solve:

x + 6 = 10

What is x?

One more:

x − 3 = 8

What is x?

Need another way?

Pick the explanation that works best for your brain.

Keep it simple

Find the operation attached to x. Do the opposite operation to both sides. Then x is by itself.

Show me visually

Imagine x is a mystery box. x + 6 = 10 means: [ mystery box ] + [6] = [10] Remove 6 from both sides. The mystery box contains 4.

Balance idea

Imagine a physical balance scale. If you remove 6 from the left side, you must remove 6 from the right side too. Otherwise the equation would no longer be balanced.

Step-by-step

1. Identify the operation.
2. Find its inverse.
3. Apply the inverse to BOTH sides.
4. Simplify.
5. Check your answer.
