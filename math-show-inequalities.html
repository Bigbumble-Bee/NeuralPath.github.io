Inequalities

An inequality is similar to an equation, but instead of saying two things are exactly equal, it describes a range of possible values. Think of it as: "What numbers are allowed?"

Visual 01 · The Symbols

First, learn the four signs.

Each symbol tells us how two values are related.

<

less than

>

greater than

≤

less than or equal to

≥

greater than or equal to

Visual 05 · The Important Rule

There is one special rule.

When you multiply or divide both sides of an inequality by a negative number, the inequality sign flips.

Remember this:

−2x > 8
↓
x < −4

The sign changed from > to < because we divided by −2.

The NeuralPath inequality method

When solving an inequality, follow the same basic process you use for equations.

1. Simplify both sides if needed.
2. Undo addition or subtraction.
3. Undo multiplication or division.
4. If you multiply or divide by a negative, flip the sign.
5. Show your answer on a number line.
